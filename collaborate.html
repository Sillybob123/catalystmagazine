How a story gets made

From a question to an edition.

Every piece in The Catalyst goes through the same four steps. You are never on your own for any of them: an editor works with you from the pitch to the printed page.

  1. The pitch

    Start with a question

    A lab down the street, a result you can't stop thinking about, a scientist whose path surprised you. Send us a few lines on the story and why it matters.

  2. The reporting

    Go and find out

    Interview the people doing the work, read the research, visit the lab. We help with introductions, questions and the tricky parts of explaining a field you are new to.

  3. The edit

    Work it with an editor

    Drafts go back and forth with an editor who reads line by line: structure, accuracy, clarity, the sentence that isn't quite there yet. Designers and illustrators take it from there.

  4. The edition

    Published, with your name on it

    Finished stories run in the season's edition under your byline, alongside the rest of the issue, and stay in the archive for good.
