The Catalyst Contributor Agreement

Version of October 15, 2026 (revised)

1. Who this agreement is between

This Contributor Agreement ("Agreement") is between you and The Catalyst Magazine, a student-run science publication based in Washington, D.C., acting through its editors and administrators ("The Catalyst," "we," "us"). It applies to everything you do as a writer, editor, social media team member, designer or other contributor, and to your use of the Editorial Suite at catalyst-magazine.com/admin (the "Suite"). It takes effect when you click "I agree."

2. Your role

You contribute as a volunteer. You will not be paid, and nothing in this Agreement makes you an employee, contractor, partner or agent of The Catalyst or of any university or organization associated with it. You are not obligated to produce any minimum amount of work, and you may stop contributing at any time by telling an editor. We may change your role or access, or end your participation, at any time.

3. Using the Editorial Suite

Your account is for you alone. Keep your password private, do not let others use your account, and tell an administrator promptly if you believe your account has been used without your permission. Use the Suite only for The Catalyst's work. Do not attempt to access pages, data or accounts you have not been given access to, do not copy or export other people's information, and do not interfere with the Suite's operation. We may monitor activity in the Suite to keep it secure and working, and we may limit, suspend or remove access at our discretion.

4. Standards for your work

Everything you submit (a "Contribution," including articles, drafts, interviews, notes, edits, captions, social media posts, images and other materials) must meet these standards:

  • Original. It is your own work. You will not plagiarize, including by copying or closely paraphrasing others' writing without credit. If you use AI tools to help with research or writing, you will tell your editor how you used them and you remain responsible for every word.
  • Accurate. To the best of your knowledge, the facts are true and the quotes are faithful to what was said. You will correct errors you discover and tell your editor.
  • Fair to sources. You will identify yourself as writing for The Catalyst when you interview someone, get consent before recording, honor agreements you make with sources about attribution, and give people a fair chance to respond to significant claims about them.
  • Lawful. It does not infringe anyone's copyright, trademark, privacy or other rights, is not defamatory, and does not violate any law or any agreement you have with someone else.
  • Transparent. You will disclose to your editor any personal, financial or professional relationship with a subject or source that could look like a conflict of interest.
  • Your own images, or properly licensed ones. You will only submit images, graphics or other media you created or have the right to use, and you will tell us their source and any credit required.

5. Editorial oversight and publication

The Catalyst's editors and administrators have editorial oversight of everything that appears in The Catalyst, on its website and on its social media. In particular, The Catalyst may, at its sole discretion: decide whether to accept, publish or decline any Contribution; edit Contributions for clarity, length, accuracy, style, tone, grammar, spelling (we use American English), legal concerns or any other reason; write or change headlines, summaries, captions and tags; choose, change or remove images and design; decide when, where and in which edition a Contribution appears; and correct, update, unpublish or remove a Contribution at any time.

We value your expertise and your voice. Editors will share substantial edits with you where practical, and you are welcome to make suggestions and recommendations at any stage. We will consider them in good faith, but the final decision about what is published, and how, rests with The Catalyst.

6. Ownership of your work

Published work. When The Catalyst accepts a Contribution for publication, you assign (transfer) to The Catalyst all right, title and interest, including copyright, in that Contribution as accepted and as edited, worldwide and for the full term of copyright. The Catalyst may publish, republish, edit, adapt, translate, excerpt, archive and distribute it in any form or medium, now known or later developed, including in print, on the web, in newsletters and on social media, and may license others to do so.

What you keep. The Catalyst will credit you as the author whenever it publishes your Contribution in full, unless you ask in writing to be credited differently or not at all, or the Contribution is substantially rewritten. The Catalyst grants you a permanent, free, non-exclusive license to share links to your published work, quote from it, and reproduce it in your personal portfolio, personal website, résumé, and academic or job applications, as long as you credit The Catalyst as the original publisher (with a link where possible). You keep all rights in your general knowledge, skills and ideas, and in your unpublished notes and research.

Work that is not accepted. If The Catalyst declines a Contribution, or it has not been accepted for publication, you keep the copyright in it and may publish it elsewhere. If The Catalyst unpublishes a Contribution it previously published, The Catalyst still owns it, but on your written request we will consider giving the rights back to you.

Moral rights. To the extent the law allows, you waive, and agree not to assert, any "moral rights" in your published Contributions, such as the right to object to edits, so that The Catalyst can edit and present your work as described in Section 5.

Media you supply. If you supply an image or other media you do not own, you grant The Catalyst whatever rights you have been given to use it, and you confirm that those rights allow the use you describe to us.

Further steps. If it is ever needed to confirm The Catalyst's ownership, you agree to sign any reasonable document that does so.

7. Your name, photo and bio

You permit The Catalyst to use your name, likeness, photo, title and biography in connection with your Contributions and your role, including on our About page, on your writer page, in bylines, in newsletters and on social media, during your time with us and afterward in our archives. You may ask us to update your bio and photo at any time through the Suite. After you leave, we may list you as an alumnus or alumna, and we will honor reasonable requests to change how you are shown.

8. Confidentiality

While you contribute and afterward, you will keep confidential: unpublished Contributions and drafts (yours and others'), editorial discussions and decisions, story ideas that have not been published, information shared by sources in confidence, and the personal information of other contributors, applicants, subscribers and readers (such as email addresses and phone numbers). You will use this information only for The Catalyst's work and will not post or forward it outside The Catalyst without an editor's permission. This does not stop you from discussing your own published work, or from sharing information when the law requires it.

9. Your information

To run the Suite we store your account details (your name, email address and role), the work and comments you add, records of activity in the Suite, and a record of your agreement to this Agreement (including the time, the version, and technical details such as your IP address and browser). Administrators can see this information. We use it only to operate The Catalyst, to keep the Suite secure, and to keep a record of our agreements. We do not sell it. Our Privacy Policy at catalyst-magazine.com/privacy explains more.

10. Your promises

You confirm that you are free to enter into this Agreement and to grant the rights in it, that your Contributions will meet the standards in Section 4, and that you will tell us promptly if you learn that any Contribution may be inaccurate, infringing or otherwise unlawful. You agree to cooperate reasonably with The Catalyst if a question or claim arises about something you contributed.

11. Responsibility

The Catalyst provides the Suite and its services as they are, without warranties of any kind. To the fullest extent the law allows, The Catalyst and its editors and administrators will not be liable for any indirect, incidental or consequential damages arising from your participation or use of the Suite. Nothing in this Agreement limits anyone's responsibility for their own intentional misconduct or for anything that cannot legally be limited.

12. Ending your participation

You or The Catalyst may end your participation at any time and for any reason. When it ends, your Suite access may be removed. Sections 5 through 12 and Sections 14 through 16 continue to apply after your participation ends, including The Catalyst's ownership of published Contributions and your confidentiality obligations.

13. Changes to this Agreement

We may update this Agreement from time to time. When we do, we will publish the new version with its date and ask you to review and accept it in the Suite before you continue. Changes will not reduce the rights you keep under Section 6 for work published before the change without your agreement.

14. Contributors under 18

If you are under 18, you confirm that a parent or legal guardian has reviewed this Agreement and agrees to it on your behalf.

15. General terms

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the District of Columbia, without regard to its conflict of law rules. It is the entire agreement between you and The Catalyst about its subject and replaces any earlier understanding. If any part is found unenforceable, the rest remains in effect, and the unenforceable part will be applied as closely to its intent as the law allows. Our not enforcing a term is not a waiver of it. You may not transfer this Agreement; The Catalyst may transfer it to a successor organization that continues the publication.

16. Electronic signature and records

By clicking "I agree," you confirm that you have read and understood this Agreement and agree to be bound by it, and you consent to sign it electronically. Your click is your electronic signature and has the same effect as a handwritten signature. The Catalyst keeps a record of your acceptance, including the date and time, the version you accepted, and the technical details described in Section 9. You can ask an administrator for a copy of this Agreement at any time, and you can read it again at catalyst-magazine.com/legal/contributor-agreement-2026-10-15b.
