Santiago Ramón y Cajal. None is generated.
The credit in the right-hand column is shown in small type in that piece's hover tag.
Sources, masks and hints are kept in `beta/masthead/source/pieces/` (gitignored); rebuild a
piece with `scripts/make-piece.py` (the exact commands are at the end).

python3 scripts/make-piece.py $S/monument-src.jpg $P/monument.webp --crop 0,0,1,0.955 --size 520 --mask $S/monument-mask.png --largest --fill

python3 scripts/make-piece.py $S/webb-src.jpg     $P/webb.webp     --size 400 --mask $S/webb-mask.png --largest --color --sat 0.78
python3 scripts/make-piece.py $S/apollo-src.jpg   $P/apollo.webp   --size 360 --mask $S/apollo-mask.png --largest --fill
python3 scripts/make-piece.py $S/panda-src.jpg    $P/panda.webp    --size 360 --mask $S/panda-mask.png --hint $S/panda-hint.png --largest

python3 scripts/make-piece.py $S/iss-src.jpg      $P/iss.webp      --crop 0,0,1,0.78 --size 460 --mask $S/iss-mask.png --largest --color --sat 0.7
python3 scripts/make-piece.py $S/robonaut-src.jpg $P/robonaut.webp --size 420 --mask $S/robonaut-mask.png --largest --color --sat 0.8

Earth, the Moon and Saturn are on black, so their masks are luminance masks (Saturn's also
fills the planet's ellipse and the ring annulus, so the night side and ring shadow stay).
The Cajal card is not cut out: crop (10, 8, 602, 708) of the source, levels 0.06–0.94,
grain 0.012, 236 px wide, saved opaque.

Delete a mask file to have `rembg` (`birefnet-general`) cut the source again.
